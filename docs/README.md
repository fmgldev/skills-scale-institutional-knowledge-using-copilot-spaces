# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme's project management process documentation. These guides provide standardized approaches to managing cross-functional projects, from initiation through retrospectives.

## Quick Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle focused on **customer value**, **iterative delivery**, **clear ownership**, and **data-informed decisions**. All processes are designed to be lightweight yet comprehensive, enabling teams to deliver efficiently while maintaining transparency and quality.

### Core Principles
- **Customer-first:** Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions.
- **Iterative delivery:** Deliver small, testable increments to gather feedback early and often.
- **Clear ownership:** Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead with defined responsibilities.
- **Data-informed decisions:** Measure impact, track metrics, and iterate based on evidence.
- **Psychological safety:** Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement across all teams.

## Project Management Processes Overview

OctoAcme's project management approach spans six key phases:

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business need, identify stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed to planning
2. **Planning** — Break work into shippable increments, estimate scope, identify dependencies, and create release plans
3. **Execution & Tracking** — Execute daily delivery activities, maintain team rhythm through standups and syncs, ensure quality through testing and reviews
4. **Risk Management & Communication** — Identify and track risks, escalate blockers, and maintain stakeholder alignment through regular updates
5. **Release & Deployment** — Deploy features to production following a standardized checklist, with rollback procedures and post-deploy verification
6. **Retrospectives & Continuous Improvement** — Capture learnings, identify action items, and drive iterative improvements to processes

### Key Roles

- **Project Manager (PM):** Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM):** Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers:** Implement features, collaborate on design and testability, write tests and documentation
- **QA/Testing:** Validates quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders:** Provide inputs, feedback, and approvals

### Key Artifacts

Across the project lifecycle, teams maintain:
- Project Charter / One-pager
- Roadmap and Release Plan
- Sprint/Iteration Backlog with Acceptance Criteria
- Definition of Done
- Risk Register and Mitigation Plans
- Retrospective notes and action items

## Communication Cadence

Consistent communication is essential to OctoAcme project success:

- **Daily:** Team standups (15 min) — focus on progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Weekly:** PM + PdM alignment sync; delivery team standups
- **Sprint/Milestone:** Sprint planning and retrospectives
- **Monthly:** Stakeholder updates
- **Ad-hoc:** Escalations and incident communication as needed
